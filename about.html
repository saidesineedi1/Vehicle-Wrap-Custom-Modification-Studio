Under normal road driving and with proper maintenance, our aerospace-grade multi-layer cast vinyl wraps last between 5 to 7 years. Every full build executed at our atelier includes our comprehensive 5-Year Studio Warranty covering peeling, bubbling, and material delamination.

Vinyl wraps are multi-layer cast PVC films (0.08–0.12mm) engineered primarily for aesthetic transformations, colors, textures (satin, matte, gloss, metallic), and bespoke liveries. Paint Protection Film (PPF) is an optically clear, self-healing polyurethane armor (0.15–0.20mm) designed specifically to absorb heavy stone chips and self-heal light scratches under heat. We also offer colored and satin PPF hybrids.
