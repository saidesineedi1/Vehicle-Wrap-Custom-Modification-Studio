SURGICAL STANDARDS

Why Discerning Supercar Owners Choose Aureon.

We refuse to take shortcuts. Where conventional shops stretch vinyl to save material, we panel-wrap with relaxed film tension, factory panel disassemblies, and deep edge tucks.

Every vehicle undergoes a multi-phase iron fallout extraction, clay bar treatment, and degreasing before entering our climate-controlled cleanroom bay. Certified master wrappers utilize high-tensile filament tape to execute precision returns without blades ever contacting your factory clearcoat.

01

Knife-Free Filament Tape Protocol

High-tensile filament tape placed beneath the vinyl slices through film with microscopic precision, banishing razor blades near your vehicle's paintwork.

02

Positive-Pressure Cleanroom Facility

HEPA-filtered climate cleanrooms locked at 22°C eliminate 99.9% of airborne contaminants, ensuring flawless adhesive bonding without trapped dust specks.

03

90-Point Optical Quality Audit

Every panel undergoes infrared post-heating to lock vinyl memory, followed by multi-angle inspection under high-CRI studio lights before client handover.

SERVICE INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Service Questions.

Everything you need to know about pricing, warranties, turnaround times, and preparation before bringing your vehicle to Aureon Customs.

Pricing is determined by vehicle dimensions, body complexity (e.g. sports coupes vs. full-size SUVs), and the chosen material tier (satin cast, liquid metallic, or color-shift films). Full exterior wraps typically start from $3,400 to $6,500+. Every full build includes full door jamb wrapping, multi-stage prep, and our 5-year comprehensive studio warranty.

We exclusively install certified Tier 1 aerospace-grade films from world-leading manufacturers: 3M (2080 Series), Avery Dennison (Supreme Wrapping Film), Inozetek (Supergloss Series), and KPMF. For paint protection, we utilize self-healing multi-layer ceramic-infused films with 10-year durability ratings.

Our comprehensive warranty covers film lifting, edge peeling, adhesive bubbling, premature discoloration, and material delamination under standard road driving. We also provide a complimentary 14-day post-install inspection and edge retightening session with every build.

A basic touchless exterior wash is recommended the day prior. Please avoid applying silicone waxes, tire shines, or ceramic detail sprays within 72 hours of your appointment. Our team executes full multi-stage chemical decontamination, clay bar treatment, and degreasing inside our cleanroom prior to installation.
