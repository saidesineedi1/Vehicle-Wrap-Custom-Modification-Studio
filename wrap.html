PRECISION WORKFLOW

Our Wrapping Process

A methodical five-stage protocol carried out in clinical cleanroom conditions to ensure zero blade contact and flawless longevity.

01

Consultation

Understand styling preferences and project .

We review styling goals, inspect factory bodywork, evaluate clearcoat condition, and determine complete coverage scope.

02

Finish Selection

Explore bespoke colours, sheens, and materials.

Compare physical cast-film swatches under direct sunlight and atelier lighting to select your ideal hue, sheen, and texture.

03

Vehicle Preparation

Deep-clean and prepare all vehicle surfaces.

Three-stage decontamination wash, clay bar residue strip, and alcohol panel degrease ensure an immaculate bonding foundation.

04

Wrapping

Apply the wrap film with surgical precision.

Cast film is tension-mapped, trimmed with knife-free filament tape, and wrapped seamlessly behind every panel edge.

05

Final Inspection

Review the finish and brief on proper aftercare.

Infrared post-heating sets vinyl memory at 105°C, followed by a 48-point optical audit before final client handover.
