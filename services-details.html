Zero-Defect Standards. Knife-Free Integrity.

Conventional wrap shops often score factory clearcoats with razor blades during trimming, resulting in invisible rust points and ruined resale values. At Aureon Customs, every cut line is traced with aerospace-grade filament tape embedded beneath the film.

We exclusively utilize high-tensile multi-cast vinyl from 3M 2080 Series, Avery Dennison Supreme, and Inozetek Supergloss. Each panel is tension-relieved and post-heated to 105°C using calibrated infrared thermography, locking the vinyl's molecular memory to prevent future lift, shrinkage, or bubbling.

Our 5-Stage Atelier Protocol

01

Multi-Stage Chemical Decontamination

Foam soak, clay-bar detailing, and IPA residue strip down inside an isolated wash bay to eliminate road grime, wax, and iron deposits.

02

Knife-Free Filament Contouring

High-tensile filament wire is precision-laid along body creases and seam lines. No metal blades ever touch your vehicle's paintwork.

03

Tension-Relieved Application

Panels are wrapped with low stretch ratios (< 8%) to ensure long-term stability and eliminate distortion in metallic or satin textures.

04

Full 90° Return Edge Tucking

All film edges are rolled deeply behind body panels, door jambs, and rubber moldings for an authentic, seamless finish that mimics factory paint.

05

Infrared Post-Heating & QC Audit

Digital pyrometer validation of all edges to 105°C, followed by a 48-point optical inspection before vehicle handover.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Authoritative answers directly from our certified master craftsmen to guide your commissioning decision.

No. In fact, premium cast vinyl preserves your factory clearcoat from stone chips, micro-abrasions, road salt, and harsh ultraviolet degradation. Because we strictly apply 100% knife-free filament cutting tape, metal blades never touch your painted panels. When removed professionally, the vehicle reveals its pristine original factory finish underneath.

Under normal outdoor conditions and regular hand washing, our high-grade 3M and Avery cast films last between 5 and 7 years. Our infrared post-heating process locks the molecular memory of the vinyl at 105°C, ensuring edges remain tight even through boiling summer temperatures and sub-zero freezing winters.

Traditional shops hand-trim vinyl using razor blades, which frequently slice through the clearcoat into bare metal, triggering premature corrosion. Aureon uses filament knifeless tape laid on the car prior to vinyl positioning. The internal ultra-fine wire pulls cleanly through the vinyl from beneath, guaranteeing razor-sharp seam lines with zero blade contact.

We strongly advise contactless pressure washing or hand-washing with pH-neutral car shampoo and clean microfiber wash mitts. Avoid automated mechanical brush car washes, which can scratch satin or gloss finishes. Applying a ceramic spray sealant every 3 months keeps the vinyl deeply hydrophobic and repellent to bugs and bird droppings.

Preexisting ceramic coatings must be lightly abraded or chemically stripped in our wash bay prior to wrapping, as the slick hydrophobic surface prevents proper vinyl tack. For vehicles with heavy stone chips, we perform precision feathering or clear spot fill so that surface imperfections do not telegraph through the newly installed vinyl film.

Our 5-Year written studio warranty protects you against any installation defects including edge lifting, bubble formation, seam separation, peeling, and premature material cracking. If an edge ever lifts under normal care, we repair or replace the affected section promptly free of charge.
