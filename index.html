WORKFLOW EXCELLENCE

Our 4-Step Perfection Process

Every wrap and modification follows a meticulous protocol engineered to safeguard your vehicle and ensure flawless fit and finish.

01

3D Render & Consultation

We digitally scan your exact vehicle contours and prepare photorealistic 3D mockups. Choose between satin, gloss, matte, brushed metal, or custom print textures.

  • Color swatch verification
  • High-resolution 3D preview
02

Deep Decontamination

A comprehensive multi-phase wash, iron particle fallout extraction, clay bar treatment, and alcohol degreasing ensures zero particulate interference beneath the film.

  • Multi-stage surface prep
  • Trim & badge disassembly
03

Cleanroom Wrap Application

In our 22°C climate-controlled bay, certified master installers apply wrap films with surgical knife-free tape, eliminating any risk of blade scratches on OEM paint.

  • Knife-free edge wrapping
  • Deep seamless tucks
04

Post-Heat & Ceramic Seal

Infrared post-heating locks the vinyl memory to curves preventing lift-offs. Finished with a hydrophobic ceramic coating for extreme UV defense and easy maintenance.

  • 90-point quality audit
  • 5-Year warranty handover
